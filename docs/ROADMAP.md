# Roadmap

## Milestone 2: Academy content
- For each of the 11 subjects: structured chapters and lessons in Arabic with English terms, worked examples, and diagrams drawn as inline SVG.
- Question banks: multiple choice with plausible distractors and per-option feedback, written questions, and calculation exercises with step-by-step solutions. Wrong answers flow into the mistake notebook automatically.
- Every lesson carries its references and a label: "general academic content". Structural content states the code, units and assumptions.
- Programming: content is generated for the language Joudy selects on the subject page.
- Content is stored as versioned data files, so updates never touch her progress records.
- Better still: if Joudy can share lecture notes or course descriptions, the content can follow her actual syllabus.

## Milestone 3: Portfolio and presentation
- Portfolio view across projects.
- Board layout composer with A1/A2 frames, placing her drawings, and export to PDF.
- Project comparison and review-history charts.

## Milestone 4: AI
- Enable once a compliant provider exists (see AI-FEASIBILITY.md). Test Arabic quality with her real drawings.
- PDF pages rendered to images on the device for analysis.
- Optional: a "solve with me" mode that links to the concrete tools.
